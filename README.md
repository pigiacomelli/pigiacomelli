## About me

I am an Economics student at the **Faculty of Economics of the University of Porto — FEP** and a software engineering student at **42 Porto**.

I build systems across **backend engineering, artificial intelligence, financial technology and reliability-oriented software**.

My goal is to combine strong computer science foundations with economic and quantitative reasoning to develop robust systems for finance, technology and AI-driven products.

```text
Economics        → markets, incentives, risk and decision-making
Computer Science → algorithms, systems, data and performance
Engineering      → architecture, testing, reliability and delivery
Artificial AI    → inference, automation and intelligent products
```
